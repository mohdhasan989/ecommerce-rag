# Shipping & Delivery

We ship to all 50 US states. Standard delivery takes 3-7 business days and is
free on orders over $50. Express delivery takes 1-2 business days and costs a
flat $14.99.

Orders placed after 2pm local time are processed the next business day. Once a
parcel leaves our warehouse you will receive a tracking number by email.

We do not currently ship internationally. Business customers should contact
support for bulk or wholesale shipping quotes.
