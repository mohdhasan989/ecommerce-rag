# Store Return Policy

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for any unused item in its
original packaging. Items must include all tags and accessories.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of
receiving the returned item. Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the item
was faulty on arrival.

## Faulty Items

If an item arrives damaged or defective, contact support within 48 hours. We
will arrange a replacement or a full refund including original shipping.

## Non-Returnable Items

The following cannot be returned: personalised items, opened perishable goods,
and items marked final sale at the time of purchase.
